Apartments in Silo on Krk island

Four modern holiday apartments in Silo, a peaceful coastal village on Krk island, close to the beach, promenade and local restaurants.

Holiday apartments near the beach in Silo

Balent Apartments are designed for guests who want a comfortable base on Krk island without losing the relaxed atmosphere of a smaller seaside village. Each apartment has two bedrooms, a kitchen, living space, bathroom and a terrace or balcony.

Silo is a practical choice for families and couples who want beach days, evening walks and easy day trips to Vrbnik, Dobrinj, Soline, Krk town, Punat or Baska.

Why choose Silo?

  • Peaceful village atmosphere on Krk island.
  • Beach, restaurants and local services within easy reach.
  • Good base for exploring beaches and towns around the island.
  • Direct booking with local hosts.
What guests usually want to know

Beach distance, apartment size, parking, quiet surroundings, direct communication with the host and easy day trips around Krk island.

Who is this page for?

This English page is written for guests who search in English before choosing a place on Krk island. It is not just a translation of a local guide: it focuses on the practical questions international guests usually ask before booking, such as beach distance, apartment size, day trips, parking and whether Silo is suitable for a quiet family holiday.

Balent Apartments work well as a base because guests can keep their daily routine simple. A beach day in Silo can be combined with dinner in the village, while another day can be used for a trip to Vrbnik, Krk town, Punat or Baska. For guests arriving by car, Krk island is easy to reach because it is connected to the mainland by bridge.

Direct booking and local advice

Direct booking is useful when you want clear communication with the host before arrival. You can ask about available dates, which apartment fits your group, what to expect in Silo and how to plan your stay around beaches, restaurants and island trips.
